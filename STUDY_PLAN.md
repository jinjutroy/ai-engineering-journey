# Evening Study Plan

This plan turns the dependency roadmap into a sustainable weekly practice. It does not replace the phase gates in `ROADMAP.md`; progress still requires evidence in `PROGRESS.md`.

## Weekly load

- Every evening: 30 minutes of AI engineering.
- Tuesday, the AI focus day: add a second 30-minute block, for 60 minutes total.
- Weekly target: 240 minutes (4 hours).
- Minimum fallback on a difficult day: 10 minutes of retrieval practice. Do not compensate by doubling the next day.

## Session protocol

### Standard evening — 30 minutes

1. **Retrieve — 5 minutes:** explain yesterday's idea without notes.
2. **Learn or build — 20 minutes:** do one small concept, derivation, implementation, test, or debugging task.
3. **Record — 5 minutes:** save one result and name the next concrete action.

### Tuesday focus session — 60 minutes

1. Complete the standard 30-minute block.
2. Take a short break.
3. Use the second 30 minutes for implementation, tests, experiments, or a failure/debug report.

Watching or reading alone does not count as evidence. A session should leave at least one durable trace: a teach-back note, diagram, derivation, code change, test, experiment result, or debug note.

## Weekly rhythm

| Day | AI activity | Time |
|---|---|---:|
| Monday | Understand: vocabulary, mental model, teach-back | 30 min |
| Tuesday | Implement and test — weekly AI focus day | 60 min |
| Wednesday | Mathematics or hand-worked example | 30 min |
| Thursday | Retrieve and debug one weak point | 30 min |
| Friday | Experiment and deliberately break an assumption | 30 min |
| Saturday | Apply the week's concept in a small artifact | 30 min |
| Sunday | Review evidence and choose next week's smallest gate | 30 min |

The topic follows the current phase. Days are activity modes, not separate courses.

## First four weeks

### Week 1 — AI field and stack

- Explain AI vs ML vs deep learning vs generative AI.
- Map data, training, evaluation, serving, monitoring, and feedback.
- Produce a one-page AI stack diagram and a five-minute teach-back.

### Week 2 — AI engineer role and lifecycle

- Trace one feature from problem definition to production feedback.
- Separate the responsibilities of software, data, ML, and AI engineering.
- Produce one request-to-response lifecycle trace with failure points.

Phase 00 exits only when both artifacts can be explained without notes.

### Week 3 — Python and NumPy foundations

- Review arrays, shapes, dtypes, broadcasting, and vectorization.
- Run the linear-regression implementation and tests.
- Compare one loop with a vectorized version and record timing and complexity.

### Week 4 — Gradients and numerical checks

- Derive the mean-squared-error gradients used by linear regression.
- Verify analytical gradients with finite differences.
- Create and diagnose at least one numerical or input-validation failure.

## Anti-overload rules

- Stop after the planned block unless energy is clearly good; consistency matters more than a heroic night.
- Keep one concept active at a time.
- If another NHỊP TUẦN activity is scheduled, AI remains a compact 30-minute base block and the other activity stays the main block.
- A 10-minute fallback preserves continuity but does not complete a planned artifact or phase gate.
- Mark knowledge progress only from direct evidence, never from reminders sent or time merely scheduled.
